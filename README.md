This is a MIDI keyboard, but much more advanced than the beginner's one. I made it because I'm pretty interested in making music (though I'm not very god at it) and I though it would be the perfect project to make that happen.
It features a full 12 note octave, and also the keys are mechnical keyboards. Also, you have the ability to switch between drums and keyboard with the flick of a switch. There are two buttons that let you lower or raise the octave. Finally, there is a voulme slider. There is art on the top left corner of Nujabes and his album Luv Sic Hexalogy because I love both of them a lot, RIP.
